PS Vita glue in newlib/libc/sys/vita
====================================

Measured in the source tree of https://github.com/vitasdk/newlib at
64aa7aa33d4f380451a1f100d19589226cdad334, the revision of the VitaSDK build of
2026-08-25 that this release links. The newlib core files are covered by
COPYING.NEWLIB and COPYING.LIBGLOSS, shipped beside this notice. The Vita glue
is a fork addition: it is not in upstream newlib.

Files with no licence notice in the source
------------------------------------------

Five further linked files carry no copyright or licence statement at that
revision:

  io.c, mlock.c, sbrk.c, syscalls.c, threading.c

They were added to the fork in 2015-2016: syscalls.c in Ilya Zhuravlev's first
Vita commit, sbrk.c, mlock.c and threading.c in his next commits, and io.c by
Yifan Lu. They were later changed by Ilya Zhuravlev, Ivan Epifanov, Francisco
José García García, Davee, Glenn Anderson, Sergi Granell, Yifan Lu and others.
They are not named in COPYING.NEWLIB. Its preamble says that, unless a file
states otherwise, the notices it lists apply to the contents of the newlib
subdirectory; whether that applies to these files is a legal question that this
notice does not resolve. No separate written permission from these authors is
recorded in this repository.

Newlib's maintainer stated on the public mailing list that files in the newlib
directory without a notice default to the Red Hat 3-clause BSD text (Jeff
Johnston, 25 June 2014,
https://sourceware.org/pipermail/newlib/2014/011490.html). That statement
concerns upstream newlib; the Vita glue was added to the vitasdk fork, and no
VitaSDK statement applying it to these five files was found.
